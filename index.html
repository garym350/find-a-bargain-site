Second-hand research, made simpler

Spot promising finds. Research before you buy.

Find a Bargain is an experimental Chrome extension that helps people review second-hand listings, identify products, and investigate possible resale opportunities. It separates research clues from verified financial evidence.

Private development preview. Find a Bargain is being tested and is not yet publicly available. Market-price estimates and profit predictions are not guarantees.

How it works

1

Review listings

Use a browser extension to inspect listings currently visible on a supported marketplace page.

2

Identify and shortlist

Filter interesting candidates and optionally use AI to identify products and flag missing information.

3

Compare evidence

Research advertised prices, product condition and available sales evidence before estimating potential resale returns.

Planned Etsy integration

Find a Bargain is applying for read-only access to Etsy's official Open API to search publicly available active listings and show relevant item titles, asking prices and links to the original Etsy product pages.

The planned integration is for price comparison and product research. It will not publish or edit Etsy listings, perform purchases, or access private shop or buyer information. Etsy asking prices will be clearly labelled as asking prices, not completed-sale values.

Etsy access is not yet active; availability depends on approval and development.

Data and transparency

The current development version supports user-triggered listing scans and optional AI analysis. If AI analysis is requested, relevant listing details and, where enabled, an item image may be sent to an AI service through the configured backend. We are evaluating privacy and distribution requirements before any public release.

No marketplace partnership or endorsement is implied. Find a Bargain is an independent project.
